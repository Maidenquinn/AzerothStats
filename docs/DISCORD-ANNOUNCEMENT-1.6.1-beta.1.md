# ⚔️ AZEROTH STATS — UPDATE 1.6.1-beta.1
**Better co-op reports, a build journal and easier run browsing!**

🤝 **CO-OP, CLEARLY VISIBLE**
Spot observed co-op runs in your history and inspect available player damage, DPS, contribution and top-spell details in the run report.

📜 **FOLLOW YOUR BUILD**
The new **Build journal** records observed spell-rank changes, equipment changes and global-stat checkpoints with before/after values. Filter by category to find what matters.
*This tracks observed changes — not exact card picks or guaranteed per-card effects.*

🔎 **FIND YOUR RUNS FASTER**
Search History and combine class/difficulty, result, co-op and favorite filters. Run selectors are clearer, and **Usage is now Trends**.

🛠️ **UX & RELIABILITY**
• Clearer official-summary vs. sampled-value labels.
• Confirmation before reducing history removes run details.
• Better text sizing, compact DPS and updated translations across all 14 languages.
• Existing history, settings and favorites are preserved.

**Quick commands**
`/azstats` — Open the addon
`/azstats history` — Browse/search runs
`/azstats coop` — Open the selected run’s co-op report
`/azstats journal` — Open its build journal
`/azstats compare` — Compare runs
`/azstats hud` — Customize your stats bar

Close the game, replace the addon files with the player ZIP, keep your existing AzerothStatsDB, then fully restart. **No history reset needed.**

Co-op details depend on native samples; other players’ healing/kills/full builds remain unavailable. Feedback and bug reports are welcome! ❤️
