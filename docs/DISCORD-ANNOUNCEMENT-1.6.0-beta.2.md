# ⚔️ AZEROTH STATS — NOW A VERIFIED ADDON!

Azeroth Stats has received permission from the game team to be posted as a **verified addon**. Thank you for the review and everyone’s feedback! ❤️

**Your runs. Your builds. Your progress.**

📊 Track observed runs, wins, playtime, kills, gold and personal records — globally or by class.
📜 Revisit your run history, inspect builds and available equipment descriptions, and compare two retained runs.
⚔️ Explore damage, effective healing, damage taken and spell/source contributions, plus available co-op snapshots.
🖼️ Enjoy the Chroniques interface, illustrated zone history, categorized heart favorites and Nemesis tracking.
⚙️ Build your own stats bar: up to eight metrics, individual scopes, order, width, height, text size, opacity and more.
🌍 Fourteen languages. Local history. No extra addon dependency or external account.

**Quick start**
Close the game, extract the player ZIP into your client’s AddOns directory, and check that you have `AddOns/AzerothStats/AzerothStats.toc`. Keep your existing AzerothStatsDB history/settings, then fully restart the game.

**Useful commands**
`/azstats` — Open/close the addon
`/azstats history` — Browse your runs
`/azstats report` — Inspect a run report
`/azstats compare` — Compare two runs
`/azstats hud` — Customize your stats bar
`/azstats diagnose` — Display diagnostics
`/azstats help` — Command help

**Still a beta:** missing API data remains unavailable; co-op comparisons depend on native samples, and usage statistics describe observed builds/rank changes rather than exact card clicks or casts.
